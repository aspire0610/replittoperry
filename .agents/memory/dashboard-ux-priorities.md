---
name: Dashboard UX priorities
description: The user's product-level priorities for the Taiwan stock dashboard.
---

Keep the Taiwan stock dashboard concise and reduce unnecessary scrolling without removing useful content. Prioritize fast AI analysis and direct, evidence-backed decisions with explicit entry, stop, and target levels when a valid plan exists. Do not manufacture certainty or prices when the available evidence is insufficient.

Keep the research office conclusion-first and compact: make the CEO judgment, status, and date easy to find, while placing secondary task details and sources behind progressive disclosure.

**Why:** The user asked to simplify the research office while keeping decisions easy to read.

**How to apply:** Preserve access to supporting facts, source links, and failed/running states; reduce repeated labels and decorative text rather than removing evidence.

Format CEO market forecasts into clearly separated, labeled sections on mobile instead of rendering the direction, backtest, current evidence, and counter-signal as one dense paragraph.

**Why:** The user said the forecast answer was too crowded and lacked layout.

**How to apply:** Keep the conclusion first, then use spacing and visual separation for each evidence section; preserve source details without compressing them into a single line.

On the AI-picks page, scrolling may smoothly reduce the title size in its normal document position, but do not pin it over page content. Preserve content flow to avoid text overlap and flicker.

**Why:** The user asked for a smoother dashboard, decisive AI guidance, and less scrolling while preserving the information users need; they explicitly rejected a floating title overlay.

**How to apply:** Surface the decision and key levels first, retain supporting facts for review, refresh market-session analysis promptly, and state a clear no-entry or insufficient-data action when evidence fails validation. For the AI-picks title, animate font size in place rather than switching to a fixed or sticky overlay.
