---
name: ETF dividend estimates
description: Ranking methodology and privacy rules for the ETF savings feature.
---

Rank ETFs by the sum of official cash distributions with ex-dividend dates in the trailing 12 months, divided by the latest official closing price. Label this as historical, not a forecast or guaranteed future yield. Save share counts only in the signed-in account's private Clerk metadata.

**Why:** The user asked for the top 10 to follow the reference image's annual yield and requested a private holdings calculator.

**How to apply:** Keep the 12-month period, latest official close, account-level persistence, and historical-estimate disclaimer aligned across the API and UI.
