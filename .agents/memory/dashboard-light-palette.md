---
name: Dashboard light palette
description: Visual direction for the Taiwan stock dashboard's light theme.
---

Light pages should use a glacier-blue gradient background and semi-transparent liquid-glass information cards. Card borders need enough cool-blue contrast and reflective highlights to keep sections clearly separated, while rounded corners and shadows should stay smooth without square clipping artifacts. Keep this visual treatment scoped to light mode.

**Why:** The user requested glacier-blue gradients and translucent glass cards, then clarified both that card sections needed clearer contrast and that corner/shadow artifacts should be corrected.

**How to apply:** Use layered ice-blue gradients for page backgrounds, translucent blurred surfaces with visible cool-blue edges and inset highlights for cards, keep decoration unclipped while cards are expanded, and do not change dark-mode styling unless asked.
